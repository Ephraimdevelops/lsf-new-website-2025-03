# Practical preparation templates

Original Haki Yangu drafts. These are preparation aids, not prescribed court forms or legally approved instruments. No filing deadlines, statutory remedies, fees or guarantees are invented. Beneficiaries should share only necessary information using a safe channel.

Mainland or Zanzibar / Tanzania Bara au Zanzibar: [location]
Region and district / Mkoa na wilaya: [details]
What happened / Nini kimetokea: [facts in your own words]
When / Lini: [dates; mark uncertain dates]
What help I want / Msaada ninaohitaji: [request]
Steps already taken / Hatua nilizochukua: [office, date, reference if known]
Upcoming hearing or deadline stated in a notice / Tarehe kwenye taarifa: [copy exactly; do not calculate]
Documents available / Nyaraka nilizonazo: [list; retain originals]
Communication or access needs / Mahitaji ya mawasiliano au ufikivu: [optional]

## 2. Evidence inventory / Orodha ya nyaraka

For each item: [item number] [description] [date] [where original is kept] [copy available?] [who supplied it] [uncertainties].
Kwa kila nyaraka: [namba] [maelezo] [tarehe] [asili ipo wapi] [nakala ipo?] [imetoka kwa nani] [jambo lisilo na uhakika].
Record facts without altering the original. Do not obtain evidence through unsafe confrontation or unauthorised access.

## 3. Appointment request / Ombi la miadi

To / Kwa: [provider]
Subject / Kuhusu: Request for an appointment / Ombi la miadi
I would like help with / Ninaomba msaada kuhusu: [brief issue]
Reference, if already issued / Kumbukumbu kama ipo: [reference]
Suitable dates / Tarehe zinazofaa: [dates]
Safe reply method / Njia salama ya kujibu: [method]
Language or access support / Lugha au msaada wa ufikivu: [request]
Please confirm availability and any preparation needed. / Tafadhali thibitisha upatikanaji wa miadi na maandalizi yanayohitajika.

## 4. Service follow-up / Ufuatiliaji wa huduma

To / Kwa: [office or provider]
On [date], I requested [service], reference [if known]. Please tell me the current status, the next step and whether you need further information.
Tarehe [tarehe] niliomba [huduma], kumbukumbu [kama ipo]. Tafadhali nijulishe hali ya ombi, hatua inayofuata na taarifa nyingine zinazohitajika.
Safe reply contact / Mawasiliano salama: [contact]

## 5. Payment clarification request / Ombi la ufafanuzi wa malipo

Use only when ordinary communication is safe. This is not a statutory demand, admission, settlement or substitute for timely filing.
To / Kwa: [recipient]
My records show [work/service] for [period] and an expected payment of [amount and basis]. I received [amount/date]. Please provide your payment record and clarify any difference.
Kumbukumbu zangu zinaonyesha [kazi/huduma] kwa [kipindi] na malipo yanayotarajiwa ya [kiasi na msingi]. Nilipokea [kiasi/tarehe]. Tafadhali toa kumbukumbu ya malipo na ufafanuzi wa tofauti.
Attachments / Viambatisho: [copies, if safe]

## Excluded from automatic generation

Affidavits, court pleadings, wills, transfers of land, binding settlements, admissions of liability and consent for child or survivor data require a separate, legally reviewed workflow. Do not invent official seals, case numbers, witnesses, signatures or claims that these drafts have legal approval.
