# Haki Yangu Product Definition

## Promise

> Tell us what happened, and Haki Yangu will help you understand what to do next, preserve what matters, reach the right assistance and follow the matter.

Haki Yangu is an access layer to one LSF justice platform, not a website wrapper and not only a chatbot. Its outcome is an understood next step and accountable assistance, not engagement time.

## Product actors

- Beneficiaries, including people who do not recognize a legal issue, use plain Swahili/English, guided intake, My Cases, secure communication, evidence organization, reminders, feedback and practical resources.
- Verified paralegals use assignments, appointments, notes, tasks, documents, referrals, outcomes, capacity and offline drafts.
- LSF staff triage, assign, supervise, safeguard, reassign, verify providers, govern content and assure data quality.
- Partner organisations publish capability/capacity, receive referrals, assign staff and report outcomes within scope.
- Donors/stakeholders see approved aggregated, de-identified metrics only.

## Modules and sequencing

| Module | Product value | Release decision |
|---|---|---|
| Guided intake + My Cases | converts need into accountable service | vertical slice |
| Staff triage + assignment | makes service operational | vertical slice |
| Paralegal workspace + secure messages | delivers assistance | vertical slice |
| Appointment/outcome/feedback | closes accountability loop | vertical slice |
| Justice Navigator / Remedy Navigator | recognizes issue and next institution | phase 3 after governed taxonomy/content |
| Evidence Vault / timeline | preserves user-organized facts | phase 3 after storage/privacy controls |
| Letter builder | practical action | phase 3 with reviewed templates |
| Before You Sign | understandable obligations/warnings | phase 3 with AI governance |
| Knowledge centre/life-event packs/calendar | prevention and education | phase 3; offline enabled |

## Non-negotiable truth in copy

Do not publish unsupported user/case counts, resolution times, “instant routing,” “100% privacy,” “official legal guidance,” “expert,” or outcome guarantees. Replace them with independently defined metrics and accurate limitations. Uploaded evidence is organized, not certified admissible. AI explains information, not legal validity and not representation.

## Success measures

Primary: understood next step, request completion, time to first human response, time to assignment, acceptance, kept appointments, referral completion, recorded outcome and beneficiary-reported resolution/satisfaction. Guardrails: safeguarding response, privacy incidents, harmful AI reports, reassignment/complaint rate, unresolved ageing, provider overload and digital-exclusion rate.
