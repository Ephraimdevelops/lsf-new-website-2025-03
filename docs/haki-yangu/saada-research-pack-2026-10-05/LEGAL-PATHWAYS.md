# Saada legal pathways — review drafts

Research date: 5 October 2026. These are original pathway designs, not approved legal advice or an assertion that any candidate forum has jurisdiction. Exact steps, forms, deadlines, fees and destinations remain unset pending evidence-based review. Kiswahili titles are unreviewed drafts.

Ask Mainland/Zanzibar location first. Do not require evidence documents before offering initial help. Safety and imminent hearing/deadline cases need an approved human escalation route. Do not use general civil or small-claims procedure for every dispute.

## P01 — Find legal aid and assess eligibility

Kiswahili draft: Kutafuta msaada wa kisheria

**Ask first:** Mainland or Zanzibar? Location? Legal problem? Safe contact method? Existing hearing date? Income information only if needed for assessment?

**Prepare if available:** Short account of the problem; notices or case reference if available; safe contact preference.

**Candidate routes to verify:** Registered legal-aid provider; eligibility assessment; paralegal or lawyer referral.

**Missing checks/documents:** Current registered-provider list; actual service areas, capacity and costs; LSF eligibility and referral SOP; appeals against refusal.

**Research sources:** BASE-LEGAL-AID, LEGAL-AID-REGULATIONS, ZNZ-LEGAL-AID-RULES, LEGAL-AID-SUPPLEMENT-2025, AMENDMENTS-LEGAL-SECTOR-2023 (see source-register.json and acquisition-manifest.json).

## P02 — Violence, sexual abuse and immediate safety

Kiswahili draft: Ukatili na usalama wa haraka

**Ask first:** Is it safe to continue? Safe device/contact? Child involved? Urgent injury? Threatened retaliation? Mainland or Zanzibar?

**Prepare if available:** Evidence only if safe to keep; medical or police reference if already obtained; no documents required to begin seeking help.

**Candidate routes to verify:** Verified emergency service; survivor support; health care; police gender/children desk; safe referral selected by trained staff.

**Missing checks/documents:** Approved emergency contacts and hours; survivor-centred consent SOP; current sexual-offences laws; PF3 guidance; Zanzibar strategy; confidentiality and mandatory reporting rules.

**Research sources:** SAFE-NPA-II, SAFE-HOMES, TRAFFICKING-ACT, BASE-CHILD, CRIM-PENAL, ZNZ-PENAL-2018, SAFE-WITNESS-ACT (see source-register.json and acquisition-manifest.json).
