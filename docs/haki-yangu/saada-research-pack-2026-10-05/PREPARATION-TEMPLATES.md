# Original preparation templates — for review

These are preparation worksheets, not prescribed court forms or approved LSF procedures. English and Kiswahili wording requires review. Do not collect completed worksheets in the public knowledge index. The app must provide a protected intake channel and approved privacy information before collecting personal details.

## 1. Short account of a legal problem / Maelezo mafupi ya tatizo la kisheria

- Where did the problem happen: Mainland or Zanzibar, region and district? / Tatizo limetokea wapi: Tanzania Bara au Zanzibar, mkoa na wilaya?
- What happened, in your own words? / Nini kimetokea, kwa maelezo yako mwenyewe?
- When did it happen? Record uncertain dates as uncertain. / Ilitokea lini? Eleza kama huna uhakika wa tarehe.
- What result are you seeking? / Unahitaji msaada au matokeo gani?
- Has any office, tribunal or court already considered it? / Je, ofisi, baraza au mahakama imekwishalishughulikia?
- Is there a hearing, notice, decision or deadline? Keep the full document if available. / Je, kuna tarehe ya kusikilizwa, notisi, uamuzi au muda uliowekwa? Hifadhi hati kamili ikiwa unayo.
- Is it safe to discuss this now, and which contact method is safe? / Je, ni salama kuzungumza sasa, na njia ipi ya mawasiliano ni salama?

Staff review: identify urgency, privacy needs, jurisdiction and issue category before selecting a route. Do not turn this worksheet into an eligibility gate.
