CIVIL APPEAL NO. 607 OF 2025 
 
(Appeal from the Judgment of the High Court of Tanzania at Iringa, Mwipopo, J.) dated the 27th 
day of December, 2024 in Probate and Administration Cause No. 02 of 2023) 
 
Appeal struck out for incomplete record (missing letters of administration) and suing respondent 
personally instead of as administratrix 
 
The dispute arose from administration of the estate of the deceased, Awardh Saleh Bader, who 
died testate in 2020. In his Will, the deceased (a Muslim) distributed most of his estate to his 
widow (1st Respondent), daughters (including Appellants), and charitable institutions, excluding 
his sons on the ground that they had already received lifetime gifts. The executor renounced 
executorship, leading the 1st Respondent to petition for letters of administration with Will 
annexed. The Appellants objected to her appointment citing age and bias, while the 2nd 
Respondent challenged the validity of the Will under Islamic law. The High Court declared the 
Will void for violating Islamic succession principles and appointed the 1st Respondent as 
Administratrix. The Appellants appealed against invalidity of the Will and appointment of the 1st 
Respondent. The Respondents raised Preliminary Objections challenging the competence of the 
appeal.

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA 
OFFICE OF THE SOLICITOR GENERAL 
Held:  
i. Failure to include the order appealed from renders and appeal incompetent. Failure to 
include letters of administration rendered incomplete record of appeal and thus appeal 
incompetent.  
ii. Suing the 1st respondent in her personal capacity instead of her legal capacity was fatal 
and made the appeal incompetent.  
Cases Laws  
i. Dr. Fortunatus Lwanyantika Maisha v. Dr. William Shija [1997]TZCA 51 
ii. Mussa Shaibu Msangi v. Sumry High Class Limited & Another [2025] TZCA 692 
Appeal Struck Out 
Statutory Provisions referred to: 
i. Probate and Administration of Estates Act, Cap. 352 (Section 52, 55(1) 71, 72(1), 
ii. Tanzania Court of Appeal Rules, 2009 (Rule 96)
