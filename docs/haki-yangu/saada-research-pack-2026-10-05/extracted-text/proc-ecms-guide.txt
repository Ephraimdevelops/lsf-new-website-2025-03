1.0 REGISTRATION OF AN EXTERNAL USER 
An external user is anyone in need of judicial services, which may 
include Advocate, State Attorney, Prosecutor, Individual, 
Institution, and so on. 
To access the system, type "cms.judiciary.go.tz" in the address 
bar, then press the "Enter" key on your computer. The "JoT-eCMS" 
form will open. 
Click the word “Create an account” as shown in the arrow in 
Figure No. 1 below

5.6 SUBMISSION OF INVENTORY. 
Once an administrator has been appointed by the court a notification 
of his appointment will be sent to his page for him file an inventory.  
i. Click the “Probate and Matrimonial” menu. 
ii. Click the " Submission of Inventory " card to view the list of 
cases waiting to submit the deceased's estate inventory. 
iii. Select the case by clicking the arrow icon. 
iv. Click the "File Inventory" button to view the relevant form. 
v. Enter the details and value of the deceased’s assets. Also, enter 
the details and value of the deceased's debts, if any. 
vi. Confirm your details by checking the checkbox; 


[PDF PAGE 32]
31 
 
vii. Click the " Save Inventory " button to submit the 
deceased's estate inventory. 
5.7 SUBMISSION OF FINAL ACCOUNT 
After submitting the inventory of the estate, you will receive a closing 
call that will demand you to submit the final accounts.  
i. Click the “Probate and Matrimonial” menu to see windows. 
ii. Click the "Submission of Final Accounts" window to view the 
list of cases waiting to submit the final accounts. 
iii. Click the "Final Accounts" button to view the relevant form. 
iv. Enter all the details accurately, then click the "Submit" button 
to save the information. 
v. Click the " Save Receipt " button to view the asset account 
details. 
vi. Confirm your details by checking the checkbox. 
vii. Click the "Submit" button to submit the final accounts to 
the court.
