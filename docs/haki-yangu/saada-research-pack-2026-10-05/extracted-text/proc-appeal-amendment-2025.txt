Tanzania Court Of Appeal (Amendment)  GOVERNMENT NOTICE No. 49 Published on. 24/1/2025  THE APPELLATE JURISDICTION ACT, (CAP. 141) _______  RULES _______  (Made under section 12)  THE TANZANIA COURT OF APPEAL (AMENDMENT) RULES, 2025  Citation and commencement GN. No. 368 of 2009  1.-(1) These Rules may be cited as the Tanzania Court of Appeal (Amendment) Rules, 2025 and shall be read as one with the Tanzania Court of Appeal Rules, 2009 hereinafter referred to as “the principal Rules”.    (2) These Rules shall come into operation on the 31st day of January, 2025.   Amendment of rule 6  2. The principal Rules are amended in rule 6(1) by deleting the word “Dar es Salaam” whenever it appears and substituting for it the word “Dodoma”.     Dar es Salaam,             IBRAHIM HAMIS JUMA,       21st January, 2025          Chief Justice
