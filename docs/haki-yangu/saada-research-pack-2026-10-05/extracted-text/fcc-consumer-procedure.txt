30 The Commission in imposing the fines stipulated under section 60 or 
any other provision of the Act shall have regard to: 
(a) the nature and extent of non-compliance or violation; 
(b) the wrongful gain or unfair advantage derived as a result of the 
non-compliance or violations; 
(c) the degree of harassment caused to any person(s) as a result 
of the non-compliance or violation; or 
(d) the repetitive nature or continuance of the non-compliance or 
violation.
