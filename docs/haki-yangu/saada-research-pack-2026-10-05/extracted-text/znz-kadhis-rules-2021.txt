IN EXERCISE of the powers conferred upon me under section 
7 of the Kadhis’ Court Act, No. 9 of 2017, and after consultation with 
the Chief Kadhi, I, OMAR OTHMAN MAKUNGU, Chief Justice of 
Zanzibar  do hereby make the following Rules:
PART ONE
PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS
1. These Rules may be cited as the Kadhis Court Rules of Procedure 
of 2021 and shall come into operation upon being signed by the Chief 
Justice	and	published	in	the	Official	Gazette.
2. These Rules shall apply to all proceedings in Kadhis Court.
3. In these Rules, unless the context requires otherwise:
“Act” means Kadhis Court Act, No. 9 of 2017;
“Child” means a person below the age of 18 years;
“Court”		means	Kadhis’	Court	as	defined	under	the	provisions	of	
the Act;
“day of appearance” means the day appointed in any summons 
or other proceedings for the appearance of the parties or any 
other day for hearing of the proceedings;
“defendant” includes a respondent;
“form” means a form set out in the Schedule, and a form referred 
to by a number means the form so numbered in that Schedule;

“Gurdian ad litem” means a person who is appointed to look after 
a minor’s interest in a suit;
“judgment creditor” means a person having the right to enforce 
judgment for payment of money either by instalment or 
otherwise;
“judgment debtor” means a person liable under any judgment for 
payment of money or any other order;
“Kadhi” means the Chief Kadhi, Deputy Chief Kadhi,Appellate 
Kadhi or District Kadhi as appointed under the provisions of 
the Act;
“notice” includes summons to attend before the Court;
“person who lacks locus standi” includes a minor, a person of 
with intellectual impairment, a condition or any kind of 
disability to which a person is legally restricted to stand on 
his own behalf;
“plaintiff” includes an applicant;
“Pleading”  means plaint or written statement of defence; and
“Registrar” means the Registrar of Kadhis’ Court appointed under 
the provisions of the Act  and include Kadhi or any person 
appointed to perform duties of the Registrar;
4.-(1) Non-compliance with any provisions of these Rules shall not 
render any proceeding void unless the Court deems that it will result 
injustice.
(2) The Court may, on its own motion or on the application of any 
party, set aside any proceeding wholly or in part as irregular, or order 
such amendments to be made on such terms as it thinks just.
PART TWO
INSTITUTION OF SUITS
5.-(1) Every suit in the District Kadhis Court shall be instituted by 
the presentation of plaint as prescribed in the Schedule to these Rules 
under  KC. Form I.

(2) Every suit shall as far as practicable be framed for the purpose 
of	affording	grounds	for	final	decision	upon	the	subject	in	dispute	and	
prevent further litigation concerning them.
(3) Every suit shall include the whole of the claim which the 
plaintiff is entitled to make in respect of the cause of action; but a 
plaintiff may relinquish any portion of his claim in order to bring the 
suit within the jurisdiction of any court.
(4) Where a plaintiff omits to sue, or intentionally relinquishes, 
any portion of his claim, he shall not afterwards sue in respect of that 
portion so omitted or relinquished.
(5) A person entitled to more than one relief in respect of the 
same cause of action may claim for all or any of such reliefs; but if he 
omits to claim any relief, except with the leave of the court, he shall not 
afterwards claim for any relief so omitted.
6.-(1) Every plaint shall contain the following:
(a) title of the Court;
(b) the case number assigned to it;
(c)	 the	date	of	filing;
(d) the names and address of the plaintiff and or his counsel, 
and the name and address of the defendant, phone 
number and e-mail address, if any;
(e) a concise statement, in numbered paragraphs, of the 
fact relied on by the plaintiff and indicating his cause of 
action, including particulars of any special capacity in 
which the plaintiff sues, if any;
(f)	 sufficient	particulars	of	the	claim;
(g) a statement of the relief claimed; and
(h) signature of the plaintiff (s), advocate, vakil or his 
authorized representative agent.

PART THREE
PARTIES TO THE SUIT 
13.-(1) Two or more persons may be joined together in one action
as plaintiffs or as defendants or where:
(a)  separate actions are brought by or against each of them, 
as the case may be, and same common question of law 
or fact arises in all the actions; and
(b)  all rights to relief claimed in the action, whether they 
are joint, several or in the alternative, are in respect of or 
arise out of the same transaction or series of transactions.
(2) Notwithstanding sub rule (1) of this rule, where it appears 
to the Court that any joinder may embarrass any defendant or delay the 
trial or be otherwise inconvenient, the Court may, on its own motion or 
on the application of the defendant, order separate trials or make such 
other	orders	as	it	thinks	fit.
(3) Judgment may be given without any amendment to any 
plaintiff for the relief to which he is entitled or against such one or more 
of the defendants as may be found liable according to their respective 
liabilities.
14.-(1) Subject to sub rule (2) of this rule, the Court may, on its 
own motion or on the application of any parties at any stage of the 
proceedings, strike out, substitute or add any party in such manner and 
upon such terms as may be just and proper.
(2) A person shall not be made plaintiff without his consent or, 
if the person lacks capacity to stand, the consent of his representatives 
which	shall	be	signified	in	writing	or	in	such	other	manner	as	may	be	
authorized.
15.-(1) Subject to sub rule (2) of this rule, at any stage of the 
proceedings in any cause or matter the Court may, as it thinks just and

29.-(1) Every pleading shall be in Kiswahili language.
(2)  A pleading shall contain only, a statement in a concise form

(3) A pleading shall  be divided into paragraphs numbered

PART TWELVE
DISCOVERY OF DOCUMENTS
69.-(1)	Any	party	may	without	filing	any	affidavit,	apply	to	the	court	
for an order directing any other party to any suit to make discovery of 
the document which is or has been in his possession, relating to any 
matter in question therein.
(2) In the hearing of such application the court may either refuse 
or	 adjourn	 the	 same,	 if	 satisfied	that	 such	 discovery	 is	 not	 necessary	
or not necessary at that stage of the suit, or make such order either 
generally or limited to certain classes of documents, as may, in his 
discretion,	be	thought	fit.
(3) Such discovery shall not be ordered when and so far as the 
court shall be of the opinion that it is not necessary either for disposing 
fairly of the suit or for saving costs.
(4) It shall be lawful for the Court, at any time during the 
pendency of any suit to order the production by any party thereto, upon 
oath, of such  documents in his possession or power, relating to any 
matter in question in such suit, as the court shall think right; and the 
court may deal with such documents, when produced, in such manner 
as shall appear just.

[PDF PAGE 34]
942
70. Any party shall be entitled to present a copy of any document 
in the possession  of another party and referred to in any pleading, 
affidavit	or	other	documents	filed	by	him	in	the	proceedings	or	on	oral	
examination.
71.-(1)  Where any party intentionally fails to comply with any order 
for discovery of document, he shall if a plaintiff, be liable to have his 
suit dismissed for want of prosecution, and if a defendant, to have his 
defence, if any, struck out, and to be placed in the same position as if 
he has not defended, and the party seeking discovery  may apply to the 
court for an order to that effect, and an order may be made accordingly.
(2) The Court shall not struck out the defendant's defence unless 
it is proved that the content of the alleged document to be produced 
relate to the matter in dispute and is a necessary document for proving 
or disproving the matter in dispute.
72. Production and inspection of documents under this Part shall
be subject to the provisions of any law relating to privileged 
communications and documents.
73. There shall be no appeal against any order of the Court under 
this Part except in an appeal against the judgment of the case as a whole.
PART THIRTEEN
TRANSFER OF PROCEEDINGS
74.-(1) Where a Kadhi foresees that any proceedings in that Court 
can be more conveniently or fairly tried in some other Court or Kadhi, 
he shall inform the Kadhi incharge or Registrar who shall nominate 
another Kadhi to try the case.
(2) Any transfer of any proceedings under sub rule (1) of this 
rule, may be made by the Kadhi of his own motion or on the application

PART SIXTEEN
DEATH OF PARTIES AND ABATEMENT OF SUIT
102.-(1)The death of the party shall not cause the suit to abate if the 
right to sue survives.
(2) Where any of the parties dies, and where the right to 
sue survives to the surviving plaintiff alone, or against the surviving 
defendant alone, the court shall cause an entry to that effect to be made 
on the record, and the suit shall proceed at the instance of the surviving 
plaintiff or against the surviving defendant.
(3) Where a sole plaintiff dies, and the right to sue survives, 
the court, on an application made in that behalf, shall cause the legal 
representative of the deceased plaintiff to be made a party and shall 
proceed with the suit.
(4) Where within sixty days from the death of the plaintiff no 
application is made under sub rule (3) of this rule, the suit shall abate 
so far as the deceased plaintiff is concerned, and, on the application of 
the defendant, the court may award to him the costs which he may have 
incurred in  defending the suit to be recovered from the estate of the 
deceased plaintiff.

the	act	to	be	done	within	such	time	as	may	be	specified	therein.
(2) Where a judgment or an order requiring a person to do 
an act does not specify the time within which the act is to be done,the 
Court shall have the power subsequently to make an order requiring the 
act	to	be	done	within	such	time	as	may	be	specified	therein.
124.-(1) Any person, not being a party to a cause or matter, who 
obtains an order or in whose favour an order is made, shall be entitled 
to enforce obedience to the order by the same process as if he were a 
party in the cause or matter.
(2) Any person, not being a party to a cause or matter, against 
whom obedience to any judgment or order may be enforced, shall be 
liable to the same process for enforcing obedience to the judgment or 
order as if he were a party in the cause or matter.

MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS
131. Where at any stage of the case there is a written or oral  request

an abuse of the process of the Court.
132.  In the event of a lacunae or where any matter is not expressly 
provided for in these Rules, the Court shall subject to Islamic shariah, 
adopt rules concerning such a matter under Civil Procedure Rules of 
the Laws of Zanzibar.
