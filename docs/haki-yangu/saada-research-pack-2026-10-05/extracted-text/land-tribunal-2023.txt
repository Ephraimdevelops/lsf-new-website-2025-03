     GOVERNMENT NOTICE  No. 171 published on. 17/3/2023   THE LAND DISPUTES COURTS ACT (CAP. 216) _______  REGULATIONS _______  (Made under section 56) _______   THE LAND DISPUTES COURTS (THE DISTRICT LAND AND HOUSING TRIBUNAL) (AMENDMENT), REGULATIONS, 2023  Citation     GN. No.174 of 2003 
 1. These Regulations may be cited as The Land Disputes Courts (The District Land and Housing Tribunal) (Amendment) Regulations, 2023 and shall be read as one with The Land Disputes Courts (The District Land and Housing Tribunal), Regulations 2003 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Regulations”.   Amendment of regulation 2  2. The principal Regulations are amended in regulation 2 by deleting the definition of the terms “committee”, “process server” and “tribunal broker”. Amendment of regulation 5  3. The principal Regulations are amended in regulation 5 (a) by adding immediately after the word “mentioned” the words “ through the Form No.2 as prescribed in the Second Schedule to these Regulations”.   Amendment of regulations 26,27,28,29,30,3 4. The principal Regulations are amended by deleting regulations 26, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31 and 32.

The principal Regulations are amended by deleting regulation 35 and substituting for it the following:  “35. The Appointing Authority may, upon receipt of recommendations from the Registrar, remove the Chairman from the office”.   Amendment of regulation 36  7. The principal Regulations are amended in regulation 36(2) by deleting the words “Fifth Schedule” and substituting for it the words “Third Schedule”.   Amendment of First Schedule  8. The principal Regulations are amended in the First Schedule by- (a) deleting item number 8, 14 and15; and  (b) renumbering item number 9,10,11, 12, 13, 16,17,18 and 19 as number 8,9,10,11,12,13,14, 15 and 16.
