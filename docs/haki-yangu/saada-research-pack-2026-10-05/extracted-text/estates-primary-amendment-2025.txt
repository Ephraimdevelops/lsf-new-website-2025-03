Citation 
 
 
GN. No.  
49 of 1971 
 
 1. These Rules may be cited as the Primary Courts 
(Administration of Estates) (Amendment) Rules, 2025 and 
shall be read as one with the Primary Courts (Administration 
of Estates) Rules, 1971 hereinafter referred to as the 
“principal Rules”. 
  
Amendment 
of rule 2 
 2. The principal Rules are amended in rule 2 by 
inserting in the appropriate alphabetical order the following 
definition:  
 ““special mirathi account” means a bank 
account opened by an administrator 
for the purpose of collecting monies 
forming part of the estate of the 
deceased;”. 
  
Deletion and 
substitution 
of rule 3 
 3. The principal Rules are amended by deleting rule 
3 and substituting for it the following: 
 “Application  3.-(1) An application for the 
appointment of administrator under 
paragraph 2(a) or 2(b) of the Fifth 
Schedule to the Act, shall be made in 
FOMU MIRATHI-1. 
   (2)  Where an application is 
for the first time made after 
expiration of twelve months from the 
death of the deceased, the application

shall contain a statement explaining 
the delay. 
   (3) Where the explanation in 
the application is unsatisfactory, the 
court may require such further proof 
of the alleged cause of delay as it 
may think fit. 
   (4) An application under this 
rule shall be accompanied by a death 
certificate issued by a competent 
authority. 
   (5) Where for any reason 
beyond the control of the petitioner, 
a death certificate is not available, 
the petitioner shall file a burial 
permit issued by a competent 
authority instead of death certificate, 
an affidavit from a person who saw 
the remains of t he deceased being 
interred or cremated or an affidavit 
from a medical practitioner, duly 
registered, who pronounced the 
death of the deceased or any other 
affidavit which, in the opinion of the 
court, is sufficient to  prove the death 
of the deceased.”. 
  
Amendment 
of rule 4 
4. The principal Rules are amended in rule 4 by 
deleting subrule (3) and substituting for it the following:  
  “(3) If the applicant delivers to the 
officer of the court a document which he 
claims to be the Will of the deceased person, 
or to be the terms of such Will, the officer 
shall forthwith make a copy thereof, place it 
in safe custody, and require the applica nt to 
produce the original on the hearing date.”

Amendment 
of rule 5 
5. The principal Rules are amended in rule 5 by-  
(a) deleting the words “the appropriate Form” 
appearing in subrule (2) and substituting for them 
the words “FOMU MIRATHI-2;  
 (b) adding immediately after subrule (4) the 
following:  
  “(5) No grant shall be made until the 
expiration of fourteen days from the date of 
the last publication. 
 (6) Where an objection has been made 
by any interested person, the court shall 
record such objection, call upon the applicant 
to substantiate his application, hear the 
objector and determine whether or not to 
grant the application.”.

 (c) in subrule (3), by inserting the words “in FOMU 
MIRATHI-3 set out in the Schedule” between the

  “(2) The revocation or annulment of a

 (c) by renumbering subrule (2) as subrule (3).

8. The principal Rules are amended by deleting rule 10 and 
substituting for it the following: 
 “Inventory 
and accounts 
of estate 
 10.-(1) Within three months of 
the grant of administration or within 
such further period of time as the court 
may allow, the administrator shall 
submit to the court- 
  (a) a true and complete 
statement, in FOMU 
MIRATHI-5 set out in the 
Schedule, of all the assets 
and liabilities of the 
deceased person’s estate; 
and 
  (b) accounts of the estate in 
FOMU MIRATHI-6 set out 
in the Schedule showing 
therein- 
  (i) the assets which 
have come to his 
hands; 
  (ii) the expenses 
necessarily incurred; 
and  
  (iii) the residue, together 
with a statement 
showing in what 
proportion and to 
whom the residue is 
proposed to be paid 
or distributed. 
   (2) Unless the court orders 
otherwise, upon exhibition of an 
inventory and accounts, the 
administrator shall cause heirs and 
beneficiaries of the estate to appear in 
court for confirmation of inventory and 
accounts on the date fixed for such 
purpose.

Addition of 
rules 10A 
and 10B 
 9. The principal Rules are amended by adding 
immediately after rule 10 the following:  
 “Objection, 
confirmation 
and closure 
 10A.-(1) Where any person 
with interest in the estate of the 
deceased is dissatisfied with the 
inventory or accounts, he may raise an 
objection to the court orally or in 
writing before confirmation of such 
inventory or accounts. 
   (2) Where an objection is made, 
the court shall hear the parties on the 
objection, determine it and give 
directions to the administrator or 
confirm the inventory or accounts of 
estate. 
   (3) Upon confirmation of 
accounts, the court shall direct the 
administrator to distribute the estate 
according to the accounts and perfom 
all acts necessary thereto and report on 
completion thereof within the specified 
time.  
   (4) Where the report is made 
under subrule (3), the administration of 
estate shall be marked closed: 
   Provided that, no 
administration cause shall be marked 
closed unless the court is satisfied that 
all properties have been transferred to 
the rightful heirs or beneficiaries, and 
there is no any known pending 
litigation against the estate.

  Collection of 
deceased’s 
monies and 
payment 
procedure 
 10B.-(1) Upon appointment, 
the administrator shall open a special 
mirathi account and avail its details to 
the court. 
   (2) Subject to any other written 
laws, all monies forming part of the 
estate of the deceased person shall be 
deposited into a special mirathi 
account. 
    (3) Where circumstance 
requires, the court shall, through 
FOMU MIRATHI -9 set out in the 
Schedule, facilitate the administrator to 
transfer monies into the special mirathi 
account. 
    (4) Upon transfer of the 
deceased’s monies into special mirathi 
account, the transferor shall submit to 
court confirmation of such transfer 
which shall be accompanied by a 
statement of such account from the date 
of the death of the deceased. 
    (5) Monies shall not be paid 
from the special mirathi account except 
by an order of the court. 
    (6) The administrator shall 
provide the details of bank accounts of 
the heirs and beneficiaries in FOMU 
MIRATHI-6. 
     (7)The special mirathi account 
shall be closed following the 
confirmation of accounts and the 
transfer of funds to the respective heirs 
or beneficiaries, in accordance with the 
account of estate of the deceased.”.
