 1. This Order may be cited as the Executive Agencies (Tanzania Forest Services Agency) (Establishment) (Amendment) Order, 2023 and shall be read as one with the Executive Agencies (Tanzania Forest Services Agency) (Establishment) Order, 2010 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Order”.   Deletion and replacement of paragraph 2  2. The principal Order is amended by deleting paragraph 2 and substituting for it the following:   “Establishment of Tanzania Forest Services Agency  
  “2. There is hereby established an Executive Agency to be known as Tanzania Forest Services Agency in its acronym “TFS” to manage national forest reserves, bee reserves, forest on general land and other functions as specified in the forest and beekeeping laws.”.   Addition of paragraph 2A  3. The principal Order is amended by adding immediately after paragraph 2 the following:    “Functions of Tanzania Forest Services Agency  2A. The functions of Tanzania Forest Services Agency shall be to- (a) provide forestry and
