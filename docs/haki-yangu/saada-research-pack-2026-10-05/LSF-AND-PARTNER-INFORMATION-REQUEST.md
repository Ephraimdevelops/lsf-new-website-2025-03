# Information LSF and partners must supply or approve

Prepared for review,5 October2026. This is a document request and decision worksheet, not a message sent to anyone, an approved operating policy or a claim that these policies already exist. Public research cannot determine the answers. Provide a redacted policy or controlled-access reference where staff/client information is confidential.

| Owner to identify | Document or decision needed | Minimum information to supply | Review evidence required |
|---|---|---|---|
| Programme lead | Programme scope and eligibility policy | Supported issues, beneficiaries, location, means assessment, exclusions, prioritisation, alternatives for missing ID/income proof, refusal review | Named approver, version, effective date, review date; alignment with current legal-aid law |
| Legal lead | Approved34 legal pathways | Each branch’s material facts, jurisdiction, forum, exact legal provisions, forms, required/optional documents, deadline trigger/exceptions, fees/remission, remedies and appeal | Exact source/version/page or section for every operative instruction; current-law and judgment review |
| Legal lead | Mainland/Zanzibar application rules | Union/non-Union scope, mixed regulatory exceptions, cross-island disputes, personal-law questions, competence boundaries | Subject-specific law and approved escalation when jurisdiction is uncertain |
| Legal lead | Assistant boundaries | Permitted legal information, prohibited conclusions, evidence threshold, citation format, handling uncertainty/conflicting sources, human escalation | Approved examples of answer, ask-first, abstain and escalate outcomes |
| Service coordinator | Provider register and office directory | Registration proof, office ID, public vs confidential address, service scope, area, contacts, hours, accessibility, languages, costs, eligibility, intake channel and capacity | Provider confirmation, verification method/date, owner and expiry; resolve all conflicting public contacts |
| Partner owner | Referral agreement | Accepted referral types, required minimal information, consent wording, safe transmission, acknowledgement, response target, decline/overload routing, closure/outcome feedback | Signed or approved agreement; tested handoff and named recipient role |
| Operations lead | Intake and triage SOP | Questions, urgency flags, missing-paperwork alternatives, conflicts check, assignment, safe callbacks, queue ownership, out-of-hours handling | Staff training, workflow examples and escalation coverage |
| Safeguarding lead | Child and survivor safeguarding SOP | Safe device/contact checks, child participation/consent, guardian risks, mandatory-reporting assessment, emergency escalation, safe referral and record minimisation | Legal and safeguarding approval; usable local services; exercise including unavailable/unsafe contact |
| Safeguarding lead | Emergency contacts and service coverage | Verified channels, location, hours, response limits, escalation alternatives and periodic reconfirmation | Issuing authority/provider confirmation; no unsupported promise of immediate assistance |
| Data controller/DPO | Privacy notice and lawful-basis decisions | Controllers/processors, purpose per field, basis, children’s data, minimisation, retention per record, deletion/export, access rights, transfers and complaints | Approved policy, processing records and vendor/integration agreements |
| Data controller/DPO | Breach and security incident SOP | Reporting roles, triage, containment, legal notification assessment, evidence preservation, user communications and recovery | Named owners, approved time/trigger rules and exercised incident plan |
| IT/security owner | Role and permission matrix | Client, intake staff, paralegal, lawyer, supervisor, independent document reviewer and administrator permissions; tenant and case isolation | Tested access denials, audit access, joiner/mover/leaver process |
| Legal/content owner | Source reuse and translation policy | Permission for private storage, indexing, excerpts, translation and redistribution; attribution and prohibited uses | Per-source licence/permission record. Downloadability alone does not establish rights |
| Content owner | Independent review workflow | Uploader/reviewer separation, reviewer competence, approve/reject/revoke, corrections, amendment lineage, expiry, source replacement | Named reviewers and evidence of actual decisions; required app metadata completed |
| Language/accessibility owner | English/Kiswahili style and terminology | Legal terms, dialect/reading level, accessible alternatives, disability needs, low-bandwidth/offline handling | Reviewed drafts and bilingual usability testing; legal meaning checked independently |
| Geography/service owner | Place and service-area mapping | Approved code namespace, Mainland/Zanzibar regions and ward/shehia hierarchy, aliases, boundary updates, provider catchments, rural/remote alternatives | NBS/source licence, resolution of duplicate codes and projection metadata, current administrative validation |
| Case-management owner | Case, booking and status model | Case lifecycle, appointment slots, confirmation, reschedule/cancel, missed appointment, status ownership, notifications and consent | Real authenticated data source, API/workflow contracts, failure/reconciliation tests |
| Finance/programme owner | Fee and cost support policy | Court/regulator fees vs provider charges, legal-aid funding, eligible remission, travel/translation costs, receipts and reimbursement | Current schedules and approved programme decisions; avoid blanket free-service claims |
| Quality owner | Evaluation and release criteria | Legal correctness, jurisdiction, citation faithfulness, retrieval coverage, safety, privacy, referral success, bilingual/accessibility scoring | Qualified reviewers score prepared cases; critical failures have owners and release gates |
| Operations/IT owner | Monitoring, maintenance and recovery | Source review calendar, broken-link checks, referral queues, corrections, incident ownership, backup/restore, retention, availability | Named owner per task, approved cadence and completed restore/handoff exercises |
| Complaints owner | User and service complaints SOP | Safe complaint channel, response/appeal route, conflicts of interest, reporting of staff misconduct and accessible alternatives | Approved process with real accountable owner and appropriate confidentiality |

For each row record: document ID; owner; supplied file or controlled reference; version; decision; approver; effective date; next review date; implementation dependencies; unresolved questions. Leave unknowns blank. Do not fill a named reviewer, approval or deadline merely to satisfy a schema.

The public pack supplies evidence, original preparation drafts, review queues and source candidates. LSF and partner decisions turn those into real operational rules. Private client case records, staff-only safeguarding instructions and confidential locations must stay outside a general public RAG index.
