# Store and campaign copy — draft for release acceptance

Use only after the listed journeys pass in the actual release. Confirm LSF developer identity and monitored support before submission. Do not insert a Play badge or download link before a working public listing exists.

## Listing

App name: Haki Yangu

English short description: Find legal help, request support and follow your case in Tanzania.

Kiswahili short description: Tafuta msaada wa kisheria, tuma ombi na fuatilia kesi yako.

English description:

Haki Yangu helps you take the next step when you need legal support in Tanzania.

Find public legal-help services, read available rights information, and send a request to the Legal Services Facility (LSF). When your request becomes a case, follow updates, communicate with your case team, share documents you choose to submit, and view appointments.

Use Kiswahili or English. Your account gives you access to your own requests and case information across the web and mobile app. Service availability depends on the relevant provider and the review of your request. Submitting a request does not guarantee representation or a particular outcome.

Haki Yangu is not an emergency response service. General information in the app does not replace advice from a qualified professional about your circumstances.

For account and data deletion, use the website’s account-deletion page or contact LSF. Review the privacy policy before submitting personal information.

Kiswahili description:

Haki Yangu inakusaidia kuchukua hatua inayofuata unapohitaji msaada wa kisheria Tanzania.

Tafuta huduma za msaada wa kisheria, soma taarifa zilizopo kuhusu haki zako, na tuma ombi kwa Legal Services Facility (LSF). Ombi lako likifunguliwa kama kesi, unaweza kufuatilia taarifa, kuwasiliana na timu ya kesi, kutuma nyaraka unazochagua na kuona miadi.

Tumia Kiswahili au Kiingereza. Akaunti yako hukuwezesha kuona maombi na taarifa zako kwenye tovuti na programu ya simu. Upatikanaji wa huduma hutegemea mtoa huduma na mapitio ya ombi lako. Kutuma ombi hakuhakikishi uwakilishi au matokeo fulani.

Haki Yangu si huduma ya dharura. Taarifa za jumla kwenye programu hazichukui nafasi ya ushauri wa mtaalamu kuhusu hali yako.

Kwa kufuta akaunti au kuomba kufutwa kwa taarifa, tumia ukurasa wa kufuta akaunti kwenye tovuti au wasiliana na LSF. Soma sera ya faragha kabla ya kutuma taarifa binafsi.
