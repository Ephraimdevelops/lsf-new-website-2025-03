## Principles for screen work

- **Purpose first.** Lead with identity, one clear message and the next action. Keep beneficiary screens calm and linear; staff screens may have queues and metrics when they help work get done.
- **Existing brand anchor.** Retain the app's current burgundy and its sign-in treatment. Teal signals discovery, orange guidance, gold learning. Prefer pale tints and white surfaces for most content. Do not recolour the entire app or fill each card with saturated colour.
- **Hierarchy before decoration.** Each screen has one main message and one clear next action. A beneficiary home can use one human/editorial hero; the four entry choices should be quiet, readable, pale cards, not four competing photographic panels. Use one icon language for controls and keep photography selective.
- **Legibility and space.** Use the existing 24 px screen margin, 8 px spacing rhythm, generous 18–24 px card radii, 16 px body text where space permits and at least 48 px touch targets. Keep helper text short. Labels remain above form inputs. Respect the existing safe-area Screen container.
- **Imagery.** Human photography must be real, appropriately licensed and relevant to Tanzanian users. Clay-like objects are optional accents for a meaningful action or empty/success state; no fabricated people or excessive 3D icons. Utility navigation stays simple 2D.
- **States are designed, not omitted.** Each route needs loading, empty, failed, restricted, completed and filled-data review as relevant. Counts must not show zero while data is loading. Decisions show what changes, a reason field where required and the resulting status. Sensitive locations and case facts never appear in decorative or public cards.
- **Language and access.** All new copy must have Kiswahili and English. Use plain status names, accessible labels, readable contrast, text resizing and reduced-motion-friendly transitions. The main action must remain obvious on a small phone.

Shared implementation tokens live in `mobile/src/theme.ts` under `civic`: 24 px page inset, 48 px minimum touch, 16 px body target, 32 px section gap and semantic pale entry-card surfaces. These are defaults for new screen work; adapt where a small phone or long Kiswahili label needs more room.
