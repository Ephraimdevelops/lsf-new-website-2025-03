## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-01 — I was dismissed from work. What should I do first?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Act promptly. A dismissal complaint has its own referral time limit; gather the dismissal facts and check the correct labour route before waiting for negotiations.

1. Save the dismissal letter, contract, payslips, messages and dates of notification and last work.
2. Write a short timeline and identify whether you challenge the reason, procedure, unpaid benefits or all three.
3. Contact CMA or a labour legal-aid adviser promptly about referral, the prescribed form and any late-filing application.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

**Kiswahili — Nimefukuzwa kazi. Nianzie wapi?**

Chukua hatua mapema. Malalamiko ya kufukuzwa yana muda wake wa kuwasilishwa; kusanya taarifa na thibitisha njia ya kushughulikia mgogoro kabla ya kusubiri mazungumzo.

1. Hifadhi barua ya kufukuzwa, mkataba, hati za malipo, ujumbe na tarehe za taarifa na siku ya mwisho kazini.
2. Andika mfuatano mfupi wa matukio na eleza kama unapinga sababu, utaratibu, mafao ambayo hayajalipwa au yote.
3. Wasiliana mapema na CMA au mtoa msaada wa sheria za kazi kuhusu kuwasilisha mgogoro, fomu na ombi la kuchelewa ikiwa linahitajika.
4. Hifadhi nakala na namba ya kumbukumbu ya kila ombi. Thibitisha muda wa kuwasilisha, ada rasmi na tarehe inayofuata; mazungumzo ya ndani hayaongezi moja kwa moja muda wa kufungua shauri.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-02 — My employer has not paid my wages. How can I claim them?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Prepare an itemised wage claim and distinguish agreed pay from disputed deductions. The appropriate labour route depends on the employment relationship.

1. List each unpaid period, amount, hours worked and payments already received; keep attendance and payment records.
2. If safe, request payment and an explanation of deductions in writing; keep the response.
3. Ask a labour officer, CMA or a legal-aid adviser to identify the applicable wage-enforcement route and filing time.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-03 — I work without a written contract. Can I still seek help?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Lack of a written contract does not settle whether an employment relationship exists. Evidence of how the work was organised is important.

1. Collect work messages, rosters, payment records, supervisor details and witnesses.
2. Record who set your hours, supplied tools, supervised you and paid you; request written employment particulars if safe.
3. Ask a labour adviser to assess employee status and the correct complaint route; do not assume a casual or contractor label decides it.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-05 — I was injured at work. Where do I seek compensation?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Get medical care and document the work connection. Workplace injury compensation is a distinct process from an ordinary dismissal complaint.

1. Keep medical reports, incident details, witness contacts, employer information and expenses.
2. Report the incident to the employer promptly and ask for the official injury-report reference; avoid signing an unexplained final settlement.
3. Check Workers Compensation Fund requirements and applicable coverage with WCF or a legal-aid adviser.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-06 — My employer deducts pension contributions but they are missing. What next?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Compare payroll deductions with the fund statement and report the missing periods. A deduction alone does not confirm remittance.

1. Save payslips, membership number, fund statements and the employer name for each missing month.
2. Request a contribution reconciliation from the employer and relevant fund; keep both responses.
3. Use the relevant NSSF or PSSSF complaint route and seek legal help if deductions remain unexplained.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-07 — My employer refuses overtime or rest days. How do I check my rights?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Hours, overtime and rest rules depend on the applicable employment provisions and any valid agreement. Prepare actual hours rather than estimates.

1. Keep daily start/finish times, rosters, overtime instructions, contract and payslips.
2. Compare the records with what was paid and ask for the employer calculation in writing.
3. Ask a labour adviser about coverage, permitted exceptions and the wage or hours complaint route.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-EMPLOYMENT-08 — I am being sexually harassed or discriminated against at work. Where do I report?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Choose a safe reporting channel. Workplace misconduct, employment discrimination and a possible criminal offence may require different parallel responses.

1. Preserve messages and a private incident timeline; record witnesses without putting yourself at further risk.
2. Use an employer grievance channel only if safe; request protection from retaliation and confidential handling.
3. Seek labour legal aid; where violence or a suspected offence is involved, also seek appropriate police or medical assistance.
4. Use a safe contact method and share sensitive details only with the responsible professional. Do not wait for an app reply if there is immediate danger; seek in-person emergency help where safely possible.

**Kiswahili — Ninanyanyaswa kingono au kubaguliwa kazini. Nitoe taarifa wapi?**

2. Tumia njia ya malalamiko ya mwajiri ikiwa ni salama; omba kulindwa dhidi ya kulipiziwa kisasi na taarifa kushughulikiwa kwa siri.
3. Tafuta msaada wa sheria za kazi; ikiwa kuna ukatili au kosa linaloshukiwa, tafuta pia msaada unaofaa wa polisi au afya.
4. Tumia njia salama ya mawasiliano na toa taarifa nyeti kwa mtaalamu anayehusika tu. Usisubiri jibu la programu ukiwa katika hatari ya haraka; tafuta msaada wa dharura ana kwa ana ikiwa ni salama kufanya hivyo.

**English**

Preserve boundary and ownership evidence and avoid a physical confrontation. The proper land forum depends on the location, tenure and dispute.

1. Gather title or occupancy documents, survey plans, purchase records, safe photographs and witness details.
2. Ask the relevant land office to check the recorded boundary; do not remove structures or markers yourself.
3. Ask legal aid to identify the competent land mediation, tribunal or court route and any urgent protective application.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-LAND_HOUSING-03 — Can a woman own or inherit land?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

Do not accept an exclusion simply because of gender. Ownership and inheritance require review of the land documents and applicable succession law.

1. Collect occupancy or title records, marriage and family information, death records where relevant and the stated reason for exclusion.
2. Record any threats or attempted sale and seek advice before signing a surrender or family settlement.
3. Seek women-focused legal aid for the appropriate land or succession claim; do not assume every inheritance system uses identical shares.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

## KYR-LAND_HOUSING-04 — My landlord is threatening to remove me immediately. What can I do?

**Scope:** tanzania_mainland

**English**

An eviction dispute needs prompt review of the tenancy, notices and applicable law. Do not assume a universal notice period.

1. Save the tenancy agreement, rent receipts, deposit proof, messages and eviction notice.
2. Request the reason and proposed date in writing; document threats or lock changes safely.
3. Seek urgent legal aid about the competent tenancy/land forum and interim protection; seek safety help if violence is threatened.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

**Kiswahili — Mwenye nyumba anatishia kuniondoa mara moja. Nifanye nini?**

Mgogoro wa kuondolewa kwenye nyumba unahitaji ukaguzi wa haraka wa upangaji, taarifa na sheria husika. Usidhani kuna muda mmoja wa taarifa kwa kila hali.

3. Use the Zanzibar register and confirmed ZLSC/ZLS or other appropriate provider contacts; obtain an acceptance or onward-referral decision.
4. Keep a copy and reference number for every submission. Confirm the applicable deadline, official fee and next appointment; internal discussions do not automatically extend a filing deadline.

**Kiswahili — Siwezi kumudu wakili Zanzibar. Napataje mtoa msaada sahihi?**

Tumia taarifa za watoa msaada Zanzibar na thibitisha usajili wa sasa, huduma na kupokelewa. Kuwepo kwenye orodha hakuhakikishi uwakilishi.

1. Andaa maelezo mafupi, wilaya na shehia, tarehe muhimu, nyaraka na msaada unaohitaji.
2. Thibitisha kama mtoa msaada anashughulikia suala, kisiwa au eneo lako na kama kuna ada au masharti.
3. Tumia orodha ya Zanzibar na mawasiliano yaliyothibitishwa ya ZLSC, ZLS au mtoa msaada mwingine anayefaa; pata uamuzi wa kupokelewa au rufaa nyingine.
4. Hifadhi nakala na namba ya kumbukumbu ya kila ombi. Thibitisha muda wa kuwasilisha, ada rasmi na tarehe inayofuata; mazungumzo ya ndani hayaongezi moja kwa moja muda wa kufungua shauri.
