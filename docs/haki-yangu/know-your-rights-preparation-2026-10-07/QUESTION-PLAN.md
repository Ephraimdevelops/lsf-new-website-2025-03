# Know Your Rights — 72-question preparation plan

Eight original bilingual review drafts per proposed category. These are useful everyday questions, not a measured national top-72 ranking. Nothing has been uploaded or approved.

The app derives category choices from approved guides; it has no fixed Know Your Rights category enum. These nine category names are a proposed editorial taxonomy. They do not automatically change intake or matching issue categories.

Selection evidence: LHRC’s 2024 legal-aid announcement reports land (58%), family (14%) and labour (10%) as its largest case groups. This is LHRC-specific data. Official registration/regulator FAQs informed additional practical topics.

[LHRC selection evidence](https://lhrc.humanrights.or.tz/sw/news-events/LHRC_2024_LEGAL_AID)

## Work and employment / Kazi na ajira

- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-01** — I was dismissed from work. What should I do first? / Nimefukuzwa kazi. Nianzie wapi?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-02** — My employer has not paid my wages. How can I claim them? / Mwajiri hajanilipa mshahara. Naudai vipi?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-03** — I work without a written contract. Can I still seek help? / Nafanya kazi bila mkataba wa maandishi. Naweza kupata msaada?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-04** — I am pregnant and facing dismissal or refusal of leave. What can I do? / Nina ujauzito na natishiwa kufukuzwa au kunyimwa likizo. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-05** — I was injured at work. Where do I seek compensation? / Nimeumia kazini. Naomba fidia wapi?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-06** — My employer deducts pension contributions but they are missing. What next? / Mwajiri anakata michango ya pensheni lakini haionekani. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-07** — My employer refuses overtime or rest days. How do I check my rights? / Mwajiri anakataa malipo ya ziada au siku za mapumziko. Nachunguza haki zangu vipi?
- **KYR-EMPLOYMENT-08** — I am being sexually harassed or discriminated against at work. Where do I report? / Ninanyanyaswa kingono au kubaguliwa kazini. Nitoe taarifa wapi?

## Land, renting and housing / Ardhi, upangaji na makazi

- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-01** — My neighbour has moved the boundary or occupied my land. What should I do? / Jirani amehamisha mpaka au ameingia kwenye ardhi yangu. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-02** — I want to buy a plot. What should I check before paying? / Nataka kununua kiwanja. Nikague nini kabla ya kulipa?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-03** — Can a woman own or inherit land? / Mwanamke anaweza kumiliki au kurithi ardhi?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-04** — My landlord is threatening to remove me immediately. What can I do? / Mwenye nyumba anatishia kuniondoa mara moja. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-05** — My landlord will not return my deposit or my belongings. What next? / Mwenye nyumba harudishi amana au vitu vyangu. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-06** — Village leaders allocated my land to someone else. How do I challenge it? / Viongozi wa kijiji wamempa mwingine ardhi yangu. Napinga vipi?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-07** — My land is being acquired for a public project. How do I question compensation? / Ardhi yangu inachukuliwa kwa mradi wa umma. Napinga fidia vipi?
- **KYR-LAND_HOUSING-08** — Someone sold the same plot to two buyers. Where do we start? / Mtu ameuzia watu wawili kiwanja kimoja. Tunaanzia wapi?

## Family and children / Familia na watoto

- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-01** — The other parent is not supporting our child. What can I do? / Mzazi mwenzangu hamhudumii mtoto. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-02** — We are separating. How is child custody decided? / Tunatengana. Malezi ya mtoto yanaamuliwaje?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-03** — I want to separate or divorce. What is the first legal step? / Nataka kutengana au kuachana. Hatua ya kwanza ya kisheria ni ipi?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-04** — My spouse wants to sell our home without consulting me. What can I do? / Mwenzi anataka kuuza nyumba yetu bila kunishirikisha. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-05** — We lived together without a marriage certificate. Do I have any rights? / Tumeishi pamoja bila cheti cha ndoa. Nina haki zozote?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-06** — A child is being abused or neglected. Where can I seek help? / Mtoto ananyanyaswa au kupuuzwa. Natafuta msaada wapi?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-07** — A child is being forced into marriage. What should we do? / Mtoto analazimishwa kuolewa au kuoa. Tufanye nini?
- **KYR-FAMILY_CHILDREN-08** — We cannot agree on paternity. How do we address it lawfully? / Hatuwezi kukubaliana kuhusu ubaba. Tunashughulikiaje kisheria?

## Inheritance and estates / Mirathi na usimamizi wa mali

- **KYR-INHERITANCE-01** — A relative has died. How do we begin administering the estate? / Ndugu amefariki. Tunaanzaje kusimamia mirathi?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-02** — Someone is taking estate property before an administrator is appointed. What can I do? / Mtu anachukua mali za mirathi kabla ya msimamizi kuteuliwa. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-03** — I am a widow or widower being excluded from inheritance. Where do I start? / Nimefiwa na mwenzi na ninatengwa kwenye mirathi. Nianzie wapi?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-04** — Children are being left out of an estate. What should we do? / Watoto wanatengwa kwenye mirathi. Tufanye nini?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-05** — I found a will. Is it valid and what should I do with it? / Nimepata wosia. Ni halali na niufanyie nini?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-06** — The estate administrator will not explain how money was used. What next? / Msimamizi wa mirathi haelezi matumizi ya fedha. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-07** — I object to the proposed estate administrator. How do I respond? / Ninapinga msimamizi wa mirathi anayependekezwa. Nijibuje?
- **KYR-INHERITANCE-08** — The deceased left debts. Must the family pay them personally? / Marehemu ameacha madeni. Familia lazima ilipe binafsi?

## Consumer problems and money / Matatizo ya walaji na fedha

- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-01** — I bought a faulty product and the seller refuses to help. What can I do? / Nimenunua bidhaa mbovu na muuzaji anakataa kusaidia. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-02** — An online seller took my money and did not deliver. What next? / Muuzaji wa mtandaoni amechukua fedha bila kuleta bidhaa. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-03** — Money disappeared from my bank or mobile-money account. What should I do? / Fedha zimepotea kwenye benki au akaunti ya fedha ya simu. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-04** — My lender is adding unexplained charges or threatening me. Where do I complain? / Mkopeshaji anaongeza gharama zisizoeleweka au kunitishia. Nilalamike wapi?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-05** — My phone company charged me incorrectly or suspended my service. What next? / Kampuni ya simu imenikata fedha kimakosa au kusitisha huduma. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-06** — My water or electricity bill is wrong, or service was disconnected. Where do I go? / Bili ya maji au umeme si sahihi au huduma imekatwa. Niende wapi?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-07** — Someone borrowed my money and will not repay it. Can I recover it? / Mtu amekopa fedha zangu na harudishi. Naweza kuzidai?
- **KYR-CONSUMER_MONEY-08** — A contractor took payment but did not finish the work. What can I do? / Fundi au mkandarasi amelipwa lakini hajamaliza kazi. Nifanye nini?

## Safety, violence and criminal justice / Usalama, ukatili na haki katika mashauri ya jinai

- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-01** — I am being harmed or threatened by my partner. Where can I get help? / Mwenzi ananiumiza au kunitishia. Napata msaada wapi?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-02** — I have experienced sexual assault. What should I do first? / Nimefanyiwa ukatili wa kingono. Nifanye nini kwanza?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-03** — A child has been arrested. What should the family do? / Mtoto amekamatwa. Familia ifanye nini?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-04** — I or a relative has been arrested. How do we get legal help? / Mimi au ndugu amekamatwa. Tunapataje msaada wa kisheria?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-05** — How do I ask about bail, and must I pay someone privately? / Naulizaje kuhusu dhamana, na lazima nimlipe mtu binafsi?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-06** — Police did not act on my report. How do I follow up? / Polisi hawajachukua hatua kwa taarifa yangu. Nifuatilieje?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-07** — Someone is threatening me because I reported an offence. What can I do? / Mtu ananitishia kwa sababu nimetoa taarifa ya kosa. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-SAFETY_CRIMINAL-08** — My photos or personal information are being shared to threaten me. Where do I start? / Picha au taarifa zangu zinasambazwa ili kunitishia. Nianzie wapi?

## Documents and public services / Nyaraka na huduma za umma

- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-01** — I was born at home and have no birth certificate. How do I begin? / Nilizaliwa nyumbani na sina cheti cha kuzaliwa. Nianzie wapi?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-02** — My birth or death certificate is lost. How do I replace it? / Cheti changu cha kuzaliwa au kifo kimepotea. Nakipataje tena?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-03** — My certificate has a spelling error or wrong detail. What can I do? / Cheti kina kosa la herufi au taarifa. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-04** — A relative died without a death certificate. How do we obtain one? / Ndugu amefariki bila cheti cha kifo. Tunakipataje?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-05** — My marriage certificate is lost, or I need my divorce registered. What next? / Cheti cha ndoa kimepotea au nataka kusajili talaka. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-06** — My NIDA card is lost or the details do not match. Where do I start? / Kitambulisho cha NIDA kimepotea au taarifa hazilingani. Nianzie wapi?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-07** — An official asks for an unofficial payment to provide a service. What can I do? / Afisa anadai malipo yasiyo rasmi ili kutoa huduma. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-DOCUMENTS_SERVICES-08** — A public service is refusing reasonable disability access. How can I complain? / Huduma ya umma inakataa msaada unaofaa wa ulemavu. Nalalamikaje?

## Getting legal help and using justice pathways / Kupata msaada wa kisheria na kufuata njia za haki

- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-01** — I cannot afford a lawyer. How do I ask for legal aid? / Siwezi kumudu wakili. Naombaje msaada wa kisheria?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-02** — I have a dispute but do not know which office or court handles it. How do I choose? / Nina mgogoro lakini sijui ofisi au mahakama sahihi. Nachaguaje?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-03** — What documents should I bring when asking for legal help? / Nipeleke nyaraka gani ninapoomba msaada wa kisheria?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-04** — I received a court summons or notice. What should I do? / Nimepokea wito au taarifa ya mahakama. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-05** — Can mediation resolve my dispute without a full court case? / Usuluhishi unaweza kutatua mgogoro bila shauri kamili mahakamani?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-06** — I lost my case or missed a filing deadline. Is there any next step? / Nimeshindwa shauri au nimechelewa kuwasilisha. Kuna hatua nyingine?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-07** — I won a case but the other side has not complied. What can I do? / Nimeshinda shauri lakini upande mwingine hautekelezi. Nifanye nini?
- **KYR-ACCESS_JUSTICE-08** — My legal-aid referral was ignored or the service asked for an unexplained fee. What next? / Rufaa yangu ya msaada imepuuzwa au huduma imedai ada isiyoeleweka. Nifanye nini?
