## Fresh source observations

- RITA's older FAQ and newer portal show different replacement/correction fees. The drafts intentionally omit amounts and direct people to the current official channel. The service portal also distinguishes registration, replacement and correction. A divorce certificate registration is not a judicial divorce.
- Bank of Tanzania's financial consumer page requires an internal provider complaint before escalation. Its catalogue includes 2025 complaint guidelines, so older numerical time limits need reconciliation before publication.
- TCRA and EWURA have their own complaint routes. A mobile-money financial dispute should not automatically be treated as a telecom complaint. Mainland EWURA instructions must not be copied into Zanzibar utility guidance.
- ZCSRA's official online service shows Zanzibar birth/death registration and late-registration categories. Mainland RITA steps are not a universal national process.
- ZLSC's legal-aid catalogue displays inconsistent English/Swahili Act numbers. The drafts do not repeat either number as verified.
- The non-www Zanzibar Land Tribunal page returned an institutional description, while a www search result showed a directory listing. Verify current practical access rather than assuming every hostname works.
- Direct opens of CMA FAQ, PDPC home, WCF home and NSSF members page timed out in this research run. Search observations and prior local acquisitions are not represented as fresh complete page captures.
