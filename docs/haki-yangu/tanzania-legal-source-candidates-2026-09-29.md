An LSF legal reviewer must check the PDF, amendments, territorial scope, effective date, translation and reuse terms before uploading or publishing anything. Do not copy document text into the app from this list.

The official e-Library [proclamation record](https://elibrary.osg.go.tz/items/258c8605-3da0-483f-b3c9-f5389042aea1) reports commencement of the Revised Edition 2023 on 1 July 2025. A later annual supplement may change an individual Act, so the version and review date must be tracked per source. The e-Library PDFs also display a government reproduction notice; linking to the record is safer than copying whole texts into Haki Yangu until LSF confirms permissions.

Next ingestion step: an LSF-authorised uploader selects the exact current PDF, records publisher, jurisdiction, language, version, effective date and review due date, then a separate administrator approves indexing. The new mobile editorial guide flow is separate: it publishes only short bilingual guidance with a cited HTTPS source after independent approval.
