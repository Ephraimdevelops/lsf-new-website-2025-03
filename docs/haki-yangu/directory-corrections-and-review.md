# Directory corrections and reviewed information

Changes apply to the existing development backend and shared web/mobile/social AI handlers. They do not promote production or establish Instagram delivery.

## Separate approval meanings

- Legal guides retain their existing independent reviewer, version and expiry checks.
- Research can publish a bounded bilingual `preparation` or `published_contact` reference. This does not approve statutory routes, fees, deadlines, provider registration, intake or eligibility.
- Original research provenance remains private and immutable. Edits withdraw the public reference until re-reviewed. Archival, explicit withdrawal, expiry and verified source suspension remove it from public information searches.
- Public projections omit staff identities, private notes and original research objects. No provider accounts, roles, assignments or referrals are created by publication.

Manage guides in `/dashboard/editorial`, research references in `/dashboard/staff?tab=services`, and information-problem reports in `/admin?tab=sara-analytics`.

## Report handling

Explicit error feedback is bound to references exposed in the owned app conversation or consented social conversation. Confirmed social delivery records exposure; uncertain/unsent responses do not. The user's own statement is recorded. Hypothetical errors and a person's own incorrect documents are not automatically treated as complaints about an AI answer.

The AI asks one clarification when the reason or reference is unclear, records the report, avoids the disputed reference and searches alternatives. A report is not a human-takeover request. Generic answer reports retain related source references for rechecking.

Chat exclusions precede selection limits. Equivalent local/international phone formats are suppressed across alternate listings. Reporting invalidates lookup caches in the same turn. The model cannot cite an invalidated source as fresh evidence.

An admin must record verification notes and a public HTTPS evidence link for shared suspension/restoration. They can identify a specific source behind a disputed answer. The original record must be corrected before restoration. Suspension affects shared AI retrieval and applicable public directory/guide projections; it does not edit an external publisher's directory, delete profiles or change account access. A user report never establishes a replacement legal rule.

## Proximity limits

Three public area references are currently curated: Magomeni, Morocco/Morroco and Kawe. Data attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Reference URLs are retained in the source and tool metadata.

A known public office/meeting point can be compared across district boundaries. A recorded area can yield an approximate area-to-area estimate. Both are straight-line estimates from an area reference, not an exact user location, road distance, travel time or proof of the nearest person. Unknown positions remain unknown. The live LSF published contact feed currently has district/region, without ward or office coordinates; it cannot establish a Morocco-versus-Kawe location for a named person.

Do not expand the three-area table with guessed coordinates. A wider location service needs attributable place resolution, public office coordinates, ambiguity handling, coverage and freshness checks.

## Verification

Regression coverage includes ownership and consent isolation, hypothetical feedback, reported source binding, verified admin suspension/restoration, cross-listing phone exclusions, uncertain delivery, search-cache invalidation, publication scope/expiry/withdrawal, whole-word issue relevance, source minimisation and approximate proximity. Live publication counts and fictional model checks are recorded separately in the private research audit. They are not a population-level legal accuracy score or real DM delivery test.


### Retrieval failures caught in live evaluation

The initial birth-registration simulation invented a local-government-letter step without retrieving evidence, and an explicit named-provider request substituted another provider despite an available published contact. Registration questions now retrieve reviewed guides before generation, while named organisation contacts retrieve publisher information before substitution. The app can return a bounded sourced named-contact response without generation. Evidence requirements also reject unsupported filing procedures disguised as preparation. Two repeat fictional checks improved; this is not an expert correctness score or a claim that all legal topics are covered.
