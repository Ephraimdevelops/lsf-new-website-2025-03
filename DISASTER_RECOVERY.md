# 🚨 DISASTER RECOVERY CHECKLIST
**For Non-Technical Staff (COO / Executive Director)**

---

### SCENARIO 1: The Website is Down (Blank Screen / Error 500)
1. **Check Your Internet:** Try opening Google.com.
2. **Check Vercel Status:** Go to [status.vercel.com](https://status.vercel.com/).
   - If Vercel is down, wait. It's global.
3. **Contact IT Support:**
   - WhatsApp the Developer immediately.
   - Message: *"Production Down - Critical Error."*

---

### SCENARIO 2: SARA AI is Misbehaving
*(e.g., Giving wrong legal advice, being rude, or hacked)*

**IMMEDIATE ACTION:**
1. Log in to **LSF Admin Panel**.
2. Look at the top **"Mission Control"** box.
3. Click the red button: **"KILL SWITCH (Emergency Stop)"**.
   - *This instantly turns off SARA. The rest of the website remains safe.*
4. Notify the Legal Team to review the specific chat logs.

---

### SCENARIO 3: Whistleblower Identity Leak Suspected
**IMMEDIATE ACTION:**
